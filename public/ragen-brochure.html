01 — About
A software practice built for how agencies actually work
We started Ragen after watching too many agencies struggle with the same problem: great client relationships, not enough engineering capacity to deliver at the pace those clients expect.

Ragen solves this by operating as a dedicated development arm for digital agencies, design studios, and consulting firms. We handle the full engineering cycle — architecture, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance — while your team stays focused on client strategy, design direction, and business development.

We are deliberately small. Every engineer on a Ragen project brings at least five years of production experience. We hire for technical judgment and communication ability. No juniors learning on client work. No account managers padding estimates. Just senior engineers who understand both code and context.

Our model is designed around long engagements. The average client relationship runs three years. We measure success by retention — how many clients stay with us past the first project — not by quarterly growth metrics. Currently, that number is 11 out of 12.

We operate white-label by default. Your clients never see our name. Repos sit in your GitHub organization. Deployments run on your infrastructure. Client communication goes through you. We exist to make your agency more capable, not to build our own brand.

03 — Difference
Eight things that make the partnership work

Senior-only teams

Everyone writing code on your project has shipped production software before. No exception. This means fewer mistakes, better architecture, and less rework. Junior developers learn elsewhere — not on your timeline.

Direct engineer access

You talk to the people building your product. No account managers filtering information. No project managers padding estimates. Direct, technical conversations that move work forward.

White-label by default

Client meetings happen through you. Repos are in your GitHub org. Deploys go to your infrastructure. Our name appears nowhere on anything your client sees. We are invisible by design.

Long-term thinking

We optimize for maintainability two years out. Documentation, tests, and clear architecture aren't extras — they're how we avoid the technical debt that makes software expensive to change.

Predictable velocity

Two-week cycles with demonstrable output at the end of each. No "95% done" status updates that drag on for weeks. You see working software, not progress reports.

Business-aware engineering

We ask why before how. Understanding the business context helps us make better technical trade-offs. Sometimes the right answer is less code, not more.

Security as a habit

OWASP awareness, dependency audits, secure auth flows, proper secret management. Security is continuous discipline, not a pre-launch checklist item.

Written culture

Architecture decisions, trade-offs, and technical discussions are documented in writing. Institutional knowledge stays with you even if individual engineers rotate. No single points of knowledge failure.

04 — Process
How an engagement works, from first call to ongoing support
1

Discovery & scoping

Two or three conversations to understand what you're building, why, and for whom. We ask about business constraints, not just technical requirements. Output is a scoping document with milestones — not a 60-page spec nobody reads.

2

Architecture & design alignment

Before writing code, we align on database schema, API surface, component structure, and deployment strategy. We review your design work for implementation feasibility.

3

Development sprints

Two-week cycles ending with a demo of working, deployable software. You get Slack access to see progress, ask questions, and course-correct in real time. No status meetings required.

4

Review & refinement

Every PR reviewed by another engineer before merging. We test for functionality, performance, security, and edge cases. QA is part of development, not a separate phase.

5

Deployment & handover

We deploy to your infrastructure. CI/CD is configured for automated future deployments. Documentation, runbooks, and architecture diagrams included. Your team — or any future team — can pick up where we left off.

6

Ongoing support

Most clients keep us on retainer after launch. Bug fixes, features, performance improvements, dependency upgrades — the steady work of keeping software healthy over time. Flexible and responsive.

Selected projects. Names withheld; details are real.

Multi-tenant SaaS platform for a legal-tech company

React · Node.js · PostgreSQL · AWS · Stripe

A US-based legal-tech startup needed a document automation platform serving individual law firms, corporate legal departments, and legal aid organizations — each with distinct feature sets, pricing, and compliance requirements. Their prototype was single-tenant and capped at three pilot customers. We rebuilt the platform with multi-tenant architecture, role-based access control, document generation pipelines, and Stripe subscription management. Eight months from architecture to launch.

30+ paying customers in six months. Zero downtime in the first year. 4,200 documents processed per business day.

Mobile banking experience for a European fintech

React Native · Python · Django · Kubernetes · Plaid

A Berlin fintech needed an iOS and Android banking app with account aggregation, spending analytics, and savings goals. Backend integrated with Plaid for US connectivity and Open Banking APIs for Europe. Tight regulatory deadline. We built the React Native app and aggregation backend in parallel. First App Store version shipped in 14 weeks.

12,000 downloads in Q1. 4.6 App Store rating. Client raised Series A four months post-launch, citing mobile as the primary growth driver.

Supply chain visibility platform for a logistics company

Next.js · Go · PostgreSQL · Docker · Elasticsearch

A logistics firm needed to replace a legacy desktop app with a modern web platform for real-time shipment tracking, route optimization, and exception management. The system ingests data from 14 carrier APIs, normalizes it, and presents a unified view. We built the ingestion pipeline in Go and the dashboard in Next.js.

6x improvement in exception response time. 3,500+ shipments tracked daily. 70 hours per week saved in manual data entry across operations.

09 — Engagement
Three ways to work with us
Model 01

Fixed scope

For well-defined projects with clear requirements and a deadline. We scope together, agree on deliverables and timeline, and deliver against that scope. Milestone-based payment. Changes handled through a straight-forward change order process.

Model 02

Dedicated team

For ongoing product work where scope evolves. A stable engineering team operating as your development arm. Monthly flat rate. You prioritize the backlog; we deliver in two-week cycles. Minimum three-month commitment.

Model 03

Monthly retainer

For post-launch support and incremental improvements. Fixed monthly capacity for bug fixes, minor features, performance work, and dependency management. No minimum commitment. Combines with either model above.
